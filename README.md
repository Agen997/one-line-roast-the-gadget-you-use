One original line, up to 200 characters, roasting a gadget used every day.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) scores 6 candidate roasts against the
judge's 5 criteria in the brief's own order (weights 5/4/3/2/1) and writes
the winner to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

The winning line (115 characters, 1 line):

> My smartwatch congratulated me for standing up after eleven hours in my chair, a participation trophy for my spine.
